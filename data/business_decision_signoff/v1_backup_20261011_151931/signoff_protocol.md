# Business Decision Sign-off Protocol

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how to review the existing business
decision evidence and record gate-level decisions. This protocol 
does not itself grant approval or establish that the strategy is 
ready for deployment.

## 2. Starting status

- Overall sign-off status: NOT_APPROVED
- Gate review status: NOT_REVIEWED
- Action review status: NOT_REVIEWED

These are initial control states. They do not mean that all
underlying technical work has failed or succeeded.

## 3. Permitted gate review decisions

Use only one of the following values in `review_decision`:

- PASS: The reviewer has inspected the referenced evidence and
  determined that the stated exit criteria have been met.
- FAIL: The inspected evidence demonstrates that the exit
  criteria have not been met.
- BLOCKED: The decision cannot be made because required evidence,
  access, clarification, or another prerequisite is missing.
- NOT_REVIEWED: No formal review decision has been recorded.

Do not mark a gate PASS solely because a script ran successfully,
a CSV exists, a hash matches, or a previous validation passed.
The reviewer must assess the specific exit criteria.

## 4. Required record for a decision

For every gate reviewed, record:

1. Gate ID and gate name.
2. Reviewer identity or role.
3. Review date in ISO format: YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Evidence reference that another reviewer can locate.
5. Rationale explaining how the evidence meets or fails the
   stated exit criteria.
6. Any unresolved blockers or gaps.
7. Follow-up action when the gate is not passed.
8. Approval reference, where a formal approval record exists.

Do not invent evidence references or approval identifiers.

## 5. Action follow-up

The action register preserves the original action details and
proposed acceptance criteria. Update the additional follow-up
fields only when real review or progress evidence is available.

Do not mark an action closed without a closure evidence reference
and a documented basis for closure.

## 6. Overall decision control

The overall status remains NOT_APPROVED until an authorized
review process has assessed the required gates and documented
its decision.

A completed template, a successful validator, or a PASS decision
on an individual gate is not, by itself, overall deployment
approval.

Any deployment decision must consider the scope and limitations of 
the underlying evidence, including the negative full-period returns 
reported in the existing business evaluation and the documented 
policy metadata discrepancy.

## 7. Policy metadata discrepancy

The existing review package documents the difference between
the source policy status `FROZEN` and the robustness summary
status `NOT_SPECIFIED`. Preserve this discrepancy in the review
record until it is investigated and resolved through a controlled
process. This step does not alter either source.

## 8. Change control and reproducibility

The files generated by this step are new review artifacts.
The source review report, action register, decision gates,
evidence snapshot, and source manifest must remain unchanged.

The manifest records the initial generated files and their hashes.
If a reviewer edits a CSV later, its hash will no longer match
the initial build-time hash. That change should be treated as a
review-record update, not as evidence that the original build
was corrupted. Revalidate the register structure and retain
appropriate version history when recording subsequent decisions.
